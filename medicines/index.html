      How to read an answer

      Five plain labels, each with its own shape, so the answer never depends on colour alone.

      • Within the limit

        You can carry it for your own use, up to the amount shown.

      • Paperwork before you fly

        You need a permit or certificate. We show who issues it and by when.

      • Not allowed

        Leave it at home, even with a prescription.

      • Check before you pack

        It depends on your exact product, or you can’t check it yourself.

      • No set amount

        The rule gives no amount. Bring what fits your prescription.

      • Not checked yet

        We haven’t found an official rule yet. We show who to ask.
