Can I bring CBD products (cannabidiol (oils, gummies, creams)) to Japan, South Korea or China?

Checked 14 October 2026 · Independent guide by East&Well.

Short answer

  • Japan: classed as a narcotic if its THC is over Japan’s limits, so leave it at home.
  • South Korea: don’t bring it without written approval, even on a layover.
  • China: leave it at home.

Taking other medicines too? Check them all for your trip

Country by country

Japan

Check before you pack

The rule

Since 12 Dec 2024, a product is classed as a narcotic under Japanese law if its THC is over 10 ppm (oils and powders), 0.1 ppm (drinks and lotions) or 1 ppm (everything else). Japan’s health ministry won’t advise on individual products.

What to do

Leave it at home unless the maker certifies its THC is below Japan’s limits.

Sources

Japan’s health ministry: cannabis law changes and THC limits

Checked 14 October 2026

South Korea

Not allowed

The rule

Korea says not to bring CBD, marijuana or hemp products, even on an airport layover, without written approval from the Korean government.

What to do

Leave it at home.

Sources

US Embassy in Korea: controlled substances

Checked 14 October 2026

China

Check before you pack

The rule

Marijuana is prohibited in China, and we haven’t found an official rule on CBD for travellers. Hong Kong has treated CBD as a dangerous drug since 1 Feb 2023.

What to do

Leave it at home.

Sources

Chongqing government: carrying medicines into China (29 Jun 2026); Hong Kong government: CBD is a dangerous drug
