Can I bring Eliquis (apixaban) to Japan, South Korea or China?

Checked 14 October 2026 · Independent guide by East&Well.

Short answer

  • Japan: up to a one-month supply with no paperwork.
  • South Korea: up to 6 bottles or a 3-month supply.
  • China: a “reasonable quantity” for your own use.

Taking other medicines too? Check them all for your trip

Country by country

Japan

Within the limit

The rule

Prescription medicines: you can bring up to a one-month supply for your own use with no paperwork.

What to do

More than a month’s supply needs an Import Confirmation (Yunyu Kakunin-sho, once called “Yakkan Shoumei”) before you leave home. The US Embassy suggests carrying a copy of your prescription.

Sources

Japan’s health ministry (MHLW): bringing medicines for personal use; US Embassy in Japan: bringing medicine into Japan

Checked 14 October 2026

South Korea

Within the limit

The rule

Ordinary medicines: up to 6 bottles or a 3-month supply. You must be able to show they’re for your own use: a prescription, a medical certificate or the pharmacy label.

What to do

Keep them in the original packaging. You may be asked for a receipt.

Sources

Korean government notice: bringing medicines into Korea; US Embassy in Korea: controlled substances

Checked 14 October 2026

China

No set amount

The rule

China allows medicines for your own use in a “reasonable quantity”, under customs supervision. There’s no set amount.

What to do

Bring only what you need for the trip, with your prescription or medical record, and declare medicines to customs if asked.

Sources

Chongqing government: carrying medicines into China (29 Jun 2026)
