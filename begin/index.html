Plan a checkup

Begin with a conversation.

Tell us what you are hoping for. We will write back personally. Writing to us costs nothing and commits you to nothing.

We hope to open introductions in early 2027, subject to legal clearance. Leave your email and travel month and we’ll write when we do. We’ll explain exactly how we’re paid before you decide anything.

What happens next

  1. 01

    We reply, personally

    Usually with a few questions about what you want from a checkup, when you could travel and what you would like to spend. We ask you to confirm by reply before we add you to our list.

  2. 02

    If introductions open

    We write to you first, explain how it works and exactly how we are paid, and suggest one licensed partner and one accredited hospital that fit your goals. Never more than you need.

  3. 03

    A written quote, then your decision

    Every item is quoted in writing. Nothing is booked until you say so, and you are free to say no.
