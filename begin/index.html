What happens next

  1. 01

    We reply, personally

    Usually with a few questions about what you want from a checkup, when you could travel and what you would like to spend.

  2. 02

    One introduction

    We suggest one licensed partner and one accredited hospital that fit your goals. Never more than you need.

  3. 03

    A written quote, then your decision

    Your partner quotes every item in writing. Nothing is booked until you say so, and you are free to say no.
