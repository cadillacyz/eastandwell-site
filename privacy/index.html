We collect as little as we can. Reading this site doesn't need an account, the site sets no cookies, and our tools keep your answers in your own browser. We don't run ads, use advertising trackers, or sell or share personal information for advertising. We never ask for health information. If you give us your email or ask to be introduced to a partner, we use that only for the reason you gave it to us.

Who we are

East&Well is run by Joe Yang, based in Toronto, Canada. In this policy, "we" means East&Well. For anything about privacy, write to hello@eastandwell.com. We are responsible for the personal information described here.

What we don't collect

  • Health information. Our forms don't ask about symptoms, conditions, medications or test results, and we don't collect consumer health data as defined by laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. Please don't send us that kind of information. If you need medical advice, talk to your doctor.
  • Payment information. You don't pay us anything, so we don't take card details.
  • Advertising data. We don't use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or data brokers.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. We don't use analytics. If we add analytics later, we'll use a privacy-friendly service that doesn't use cookies or identify individual visitors, and we'll update this page first.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. See Google's privacy policy.

Who we share information with

  • Service providers that run parts of the site for us: our web host, GitHub Pages (GitHub's privacy statement), our font provider, Google Fonts, and, once the forms are connected, our form and newsletter provider. They may use your information only to provide their service.
  • One partner, only if you ask. If you request an introduction, we share your name, email, the city you chose and your timing with the one named partner you agreed to, and nothing else. That partner then handles your trip under its own privacy policy.
  • When the law requires it, for example in response to a valid legal order.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We honor Global Privacy Control signals, although there is nothing for them to switch off.

Where it's stored, and how we protect it

Our service providers may store information in the United States, Canada or other countries. When information moves across borders, we rely on the safeguards the law allows, such as standard contractual clauses or the provider's certifications. If you're in mainland China, sending us a form or an email means your information is transferred to and stored outside China; please send it only if you agree to that.

We keep as little as we can, the whole site is served over an encrypted connection, and only we and the provider concerned can reach what you send. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights, wherever you live

Everyone can ask us to:

  • see the personal information we hold about you, and get a copy in a common format;
  • correct it if it's wrong;
  • delete it;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to a use;
  • withdraw your consent, for example to the newsletter or to an introduction that hasn't been made yet.

Write to hello@eastandwell.com. We'll reply within 30 days and won't treat you differently for asking. We may need to confirm it's you first. Someone you authorize can ask for you.

These rights follow the strongest of the laws that may apply to you, including Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR in the European Union and the UK GDPR, US state privacy laws such as California's CCPA and Washington's My Health My Data Act, and China's Personal Information Protection Law. In the European Union and the UK, we use your information because you consent (the newsletter, an introduction you ask for) or because we have a legitimate interest in answering you and keeping the site secure.

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to your local privacy regulator: in Canada, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada; in the European Union or the UK, your national data protection authority; in California, the California Privacy Protection Agency.

We don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Children

This site is meant for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has sent us information, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use information you've already given us, we'll tell you by email first.
