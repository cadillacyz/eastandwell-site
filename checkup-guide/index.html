Most private health plans must cover these with no copay or deductible in-network, under the Affordable Care Act. Medicare covers them as well, each with its own rules on how often.

Family history rule (US Multi-Society Task Force on Colorectal Cancer, 2017): with a parent, brother, sister or child who had colon cancer, checks start at 40. If they were diagnosed before 60, or two close relatives had it, a colonoscopy every 5 years, starting at 40 or 10 years before the youngest relative’s age at diagnosis, whichever is earlier. "Last test" answers are ranges, so next dates are estimated from the middle of the range.
