06Questions

Will my insurance cover a checkup abroad?

Usually not. Most US plans and Medicare don’t pay for planned care abroad. If you are insured, guideline screenings are often free at home, and we will tell you plainly when staying home makes more sense.

Will my doctor accept results from abroad?

Share them with your doctor. Most will review a signed English report and your scan files; some repeat certain tests, which is their call.

What happens if something is found?

A doctor explains it before you leave, and every partner hospital follows a written plan for findings. You decide next steps with your own doctor.
