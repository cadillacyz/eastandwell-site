The week, honestly

Why your checkup isn’t on day one.

A long flight across time zones leaves most people tired, and a checkup is easier after a proper night’s sleep. So your checkup comes after a full day of rest, and the fast begins the evening before.

  1. Thursday

    Fly

    11:50 pm from Los Angeles, after a normal workday.

  2. Friday

    Asleep

    Lost to the date line, somewhere over the Pacific.

  3. Saturday

    Arrive

    Land 5:10 am. Hotel, daylight, a long walk, an early night.

  4. Sunday

    Rest

    A slow day. Last meal by 9 pm, then water only.

  5. Monday

    Checkup

    From 7:30 am, rested. Abalone porridge after.

  6. Tuesday

    Results

    A review where your hospital offers one. The afternoon is yours.

  7. Wednesday

    Home

    Fly back and land the same day you leave.
