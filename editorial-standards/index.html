We start from primary sources

Government agencies, hospitals’ own published prices, official screening guidelines and peer-reviewed studies come first. When we rely on journalism, industry data or a third-party guide, we say so next to the number.

Every number is dated

Prices, rules and travel advisories change. Each guide lists its sources, and each figure is tied to the date we found it. Where a price is a quote, we say when it was quoted.

We keep guides current

Time-sensitive guides, such as entry rules, Medicare, prices and health policy, are reviewed at least every three months, and straight away when a rule changes. The date at the top of each guide shows the last review.

We start from screening guidelines

When a guide discusses which tests people consider, we start from the US Preventive Services Task Force recommendations and label everything else as optional. More tests are not automatically better.

We don’t give medical advice

We don’t diagnose, interpret test results or tell you which tests you need. Our guides help you ask your own doctor better questions, and we encourage you to review any plan with them.

How we are paid

We don’t run ads and nobody pays to appear in a guide. We hope to begin introducing travelers to licensed partners abroad in early 2027, once our legal review is finished. Before the first introduction, we will publish on this page exactly how we are paid, by whom and at what rate.

Photographs and video

Our photographs and video loops show real places, and each caption says where. Photographs come from Unsplash and video from Pexels, and every photographer is credited where their work appears. Until our partners give us permission to show their facilities, nothing on this site shows a partner hospital.

Corrections

If you find an error, tell us and we will check it promptly. Significant corrections are noted at the end of the guide, with the date. hello@eastandwell.com
