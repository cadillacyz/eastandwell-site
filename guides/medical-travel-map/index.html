The medical travel map: which country has the edge for which treatment?

A first-pass map of where Americans get a clear price or speed advantage abroad, by service: checkups, dental, eye surgery, joints, IVF and cosmetic, with our method.

Based on our first-pass assessment, checkups have a clear edge in South Korea, Japan, Taiwan, Thailand and China; dental in Mexico, Costa Rica, Colombia and Turkey; eye surgery in Mexico, Thailand, Korea, Taiwan and Turkey; and joint replacement in Mexico, Costa Rica, Thailand, Malaysia and India. This is our judgment of price and speed advantage for Americans, not a ranking of quality.

How we built it

We looked at each country and service for:

  • Price gap compared with US cash prices
  • Speed of getting care
  • Quality and accreditation (JCI or national)
  • Flight distance from US hubs
  • Entry rules for US citizens
  • Safety: US travel advisory levels
  • Language at international services
  • How mature local partners are

The scores are our judgment, informed by public sources. Tell us where you think we got it wrong.

What we'd avoid

  • Unproven treatments, such as unapproved stem cell therapies.
  • Surgery without a follow-up plan at home.
  • High-risk procedures (for example, bariatric surgery) arranged mainly on price.

Before you choose

  • Check the current US travel advisory and entry rules (see our 2026–27 entry rules guide).
  • Verify accreditation on the accreditor's own site (see our hospital-checking guide).
  • Compare the full trip cost, not just the procedure price.
  • Plan follow-up care at home before you go.
